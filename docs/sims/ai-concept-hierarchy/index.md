## Description

This diagram uses nested ellipses to show containment relationships:

- **Outer ellipse** — Artificial Intelligence (encompasses all subfields)
- **Middle ellipse** — Machine Learning (the dominant AI approach today)
- **Inner ellipse** — Deep Learning (a powerful subset of ML)
- **Branch nodes** — Supervised, Unsupervised, and Reinforcement Learning (the three ML paradigms)
- **Peripheral labels** — Expert Systems, Computer Vision, NLP, Robotics, Business Intelligence (other AI areas outside ML)

**Hover** any region to see its definition. **Click** to lock the highlight and keep the tooltip visible.

## Learning Objective

Classify the relationship between AI, ML, Deep Learning, and the three learning paradigms as a nested hierarchy. (Bloom Level: Understand / classify)

## Lesson Plan

| Phase | Activity | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Explore | Hover over each ellipse and branch node to read definitions. | 3 min |
| Discuss | Which parts of the diagram are subsets of each other? | 2 min |
| Apply | Click "Deep Learning" — what larger categories contain it? | 2 min |
| Quiz | Without looking: name three AI areas that are NOT Machine Learning. | 3 min |
