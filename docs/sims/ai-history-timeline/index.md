## Description

This interactive timeline spans 1950–2025 and marks 12 key events in AI history, color-coded by era:

- **Blue** — Foundational events (Turing, Dartmouth)
- **Red** — AI Winters (funding cuts, failed promises)
- **Green** — Expert Systems era
- **Gold** — Modern ML and Deep Learning breakthroughs

**Interact:** Click any event to read a detailed description and key figures in the bottom panel. Scroll to zoom and pan along the timeline.

## Learning Objective

Recall the major milestones and periods in the history of artificial intelligence from 1950 to the present day. (Bloom Level: Remember / recall)

## Lesson Plan

| Phase | Activity | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Explore | Pan and zoom the timeline; read each event label. | 3 min |
| Click | Click each colored era to read the full description. | 4 min |
| Discuss | Why did AI experience two "winters"? What changed after 2010? | 3 min |
