## Description

This directed graph shows all 16 course chapters as nodes, with arrows indicating prerequisite dependencies. Node colors identify chapter categories:

- **Green** — Foundation (Ch 1–2)
- **Orange** — Data chapters (Ch 3–4)
- **Blue** — Math and core ML (Ch 5–8)
- **Purple** — Neural network chapters (Ch 9, 14)
- **Teal** — Classification chapters (Ch 10–12)
- **Red** — Advanced chapters (Ch 13, 15–16)

**Click** any chapter node to see its direct prerequisites and dependents highlighted, with a summary in the panel below.

## Learning Objective

Summarize the dependencies between course chapters and identify the learning pathway through the course. (Bloom Level: Understand / summarize)

## Lesson Plan

| Phase | Activity | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Explore | Zoom and pan the graph; identify which chapters have the most incoming edges. | 3 min |
| Click | Click Ch 15 (CNNs) — which chapters must you complete first? | 2 min |
| Discuss | Why do Ch 1 and Ch 4 have so many outgoing edges? | 3 min |
