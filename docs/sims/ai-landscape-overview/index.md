## Description

This interactive radial concept map places Artificial Intelligence at the center with six major subfields arranged around it: Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Robotics, Expert Systems, and Business Intelligence.

- **Hover** over any subfield node to see a definition and real-world example in the tooltip panel.
- **Click** the Machine Learning node to expand its three sub-branches (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning).
- **Click** any node a second time to reset the highlight.

## Learning Objective

Classify the major subfields and application areas of artificial intelligence and understand how they relate to each other. (Bloom Level: Understand / classify)

## Lesson Plan

| Phase | Activity | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Explore | Hover over each subfield node; read the tooltip. | 3 min |
| Discuss | Which subfields overlap? What do they share? | 3 min |
| Apply | Click ML to see the three learning paradigms. Which subfield covers ChatGPT? | 2 min |
| Reflect | Draw the hierarchy from memory on paper. | 2 min |
